Hello! Pleased to meet you.

I'm Nick, a product person, currently working for NHS England.

Since 2010, I've been been working in digital, hired by various organisations across different sectors, but always in environments where we were solving business and user problems with digital products and services. 

From innovative digital agencies such as R/GA, to big organisations such as Experian and NHS England as well as co-founding a creative tech startup I have a real range of experience and know what it takes to really deliver outcomes. 

Two-and-a-half years ago I joined NHS England, which has been a real paradigm shift for me, and as part of my new public sector product practice I'm taking the opportunity to be more reflective, extolling the values of [working in the open](https://roadmap-for-modern-digital-government.campaign.gov.uk/transparency/working-in-the-open/), and I've created this site.  

Below you will find some of my writing, trying to process what has happened in the last week via Weeknotes or when I've got something longer I'd like to think about in more depth (and generally archiving my journey!) 

I hope you will find it insightful, and mildly amusing.
