Hello! 

Pleased to meet you.

I'm Nick. For the last 2.5 years I've been hired as a Product Manager working for NHS England.

As part of my new practice and inspired by working with colleagues around me as well as extolling the GDS values of [working in the open](https://roadmap-for-modern-digital-government.campaign.gov.uk/transparency/working-in-the-open/), I've created this site. 
Below, you will find some of my writing, mainly me trying to make sense of my current role, helping me to reflect on what has happened in the past week, process my thoughts and generally archive my journey! 

I'm hoping you will find them informative and mildly entertaining, hopefully they don't bore you to death! (an most unfortunate disbenefit)
